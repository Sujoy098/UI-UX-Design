#DESCRIPTION OF TASK : 
Landing Page Redesign 
The objective of this task was to redesign an existing travel website landing page, “The Goa Trip,” to enhance visual appeal, usability, and user engagement while preserving its beach-travel theme. The original design successfully conveyed the destination’s mood but lacked a clear visual hierarchy, consistent typography, and strong call-to-action emphasis, which reduced its overall effectiveness.

The redesign focuses on creating a cleaner, more modern layout with improved structure and readability. The hero section was refined with a stronger headline, simplified supporting text, and clearer primary and secondary call-to-action buttons to immediately guide users toward booking or exploring. Typography was standardized to ensure better contrast and legibility across all sections. Spacing and alignment were improved to reduce clutter and make content easier to scan.

Service and destination sections were redesigned using consistent card layouts, clearer titles, and balanced imagery to improve visual flow and maintain user interest. Color usage was streamlined to reflect a calm, coastal aesthetic while maintaining contrast for important actions. The contact and subscription sections were simplified to reduce friction and encourage user interaction.

Overall, the redesigned landing page delivers a more professional, user-friendly experience that strengthens brand credibility, improves navigation, and increases the likelihood of user conversion.
